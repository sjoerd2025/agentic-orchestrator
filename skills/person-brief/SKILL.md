# person-brief

Research a person from public sources and produce a structured intelligence brief with clickable citations. Owned by `person-to-brief-af`.

## When to use
- A task names a person (with or without their company) and wants context, background, or briefing material.

## Procedure
1. Resolve identity: disambiguate from same-named individuals via company, role, geography, signals.
2. Research: search public sources in parallel; collect dated signals.
3. Verify: each claim against at least two independent public sources; drop unverifiable claims.
4. Deliver: structured brief per `format.md` (summary, priorities, recent signals, conversation starters, messaging angles, cautions) with a clickable citation on every claim.

## Non-negotiables
- Never fabricate sources, quotes, or dates.
- If no research tool is available, say so and deliver the structure with only what is verifiable.

## Output
`person-brief.md` (+ optional brief.pdf with the same content and citations) in `format.md`'s shape.
